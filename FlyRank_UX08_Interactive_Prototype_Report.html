2. Advanced Motion & Interaction Choreography

Interaction Figma Trigger & Action Motion Curve & Timing Heuristic Payoff
Tactile Slider Quick-Snap On Click [ 50% ] → Swap Variant (325 Units) Smart Animate Spring: Mass: 1, Stiff: 180, Damp: 24 (250ms) Tactile physical weight; eliminates abrupt visual jumps.
Centered Modal Overlay On Click [ Rebalance ] → Open Overlay MOD-02 Ease-Out Quintic: 300ms entry; 60% slate backdrop Focuses 100% of attention on emergency triage (Von Restorff).
Explainable AI Drawer On Click [ ℹ️ XAI Math ] → Expand Accordion Ease-In-Out Quadratic: 180ms height transition Progressive Disclosure; reveals formula without leaving modal.
Persistent 60s Undo Ring After Delay (200ms) → Display Floating BAN-01 Linear 60s Countdown: SVG stroke offset ring Eliminates fear of accidental commit; user control & freedom.

3. Multi-Device Self-Testing & Bug Fixes Log

Device Class Interaction Breakdown Detected Implemented Layout / Prototype Fix
Desktop (1440px) Modal overlay was locked; clicking outside or pressing Esc failed to close, trapping the user. Enabled "Close when clicking outside" and mapped keyboard shortcut Esc as an explicit overlay close trigger.
Tablet Touch (1024px) Quick-snap pills were too thin (30px), causing finger mis-taps on the adjacent slider thumb. Enlarged hit-boxes to 44px × 48px touch targets with 8px thumb spacing (Fitts's Law).
All Viewports The 60s Undo Toast rendered at bottom-right, colliding with the primary action button bar. Re-anchored toast to floating Bottom-Center (+24px offset), ensuring zero visual overlap with critical buttons.
