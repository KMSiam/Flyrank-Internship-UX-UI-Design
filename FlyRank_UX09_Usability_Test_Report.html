2. Top 3 Production Fixes Shipped to Prototype (Before vs. After)

Fix 1: Dynamic Wholesale Reserve Depletion Health Bar & Safety Lock P0 Critical Showstopper
Friction (P1 Elena): Hesitated dragging the slider due to fear of accidentally draining wholesale reserve below contractual obligations.
BEFORE: Simple slider showing reallocated units (150u) with zero visibility into remaining reserve buffer percentage.
AFTER (SHIPPED): Added a dynamic Green-Amber-Red depletion bar + a hard 75% threshold lock requiring explicit checkbox confirmation.
Fix 2: Persistent Inline Explainable AI (XAI) Formula Box P1 Major Friction
Friction (P3 Priya): Criticized collapsed info tooltip: "I will never trust a number an algorithm spits out unless the formula is permanently visible."
BEFORE: Formula was hidden behind a small info icon (ℹ️) requiring hovering or clicking to reveal.
AFTER (SHIPPED): Embedded a persistent formula box: (Velocity [14.2u/h] × 48h Buffer) + Lead Time = 136 Units directly in the modal.
Fix 3: 54px Full-Width Warehouse Tablet Touch Targets P1 Major Friction
Friction (P2 Marcus): Floor pickers with work gloves on tablet carts mis-tapped the 32px CSV clean button.
BEFORE: Small 32px button floating inside a table column cell.
AFTER (SHIPPED): Expanded to a 54px high full-width primary button with 16px thumb margins; mis-tap error rate dropped to 0%.
