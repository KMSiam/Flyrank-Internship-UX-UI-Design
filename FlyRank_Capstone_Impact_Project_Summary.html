1. Project Snapshot (Honest Reality for Incoming Designers)

Core Problem: Multi-channel DTC brands scaling on Shopify, TikTok Shop, and Amazon FBA suffer a 42-minute batch inventory sync lag. Viral demand spikes deplete stock on one channel while others oversell phantom units, causing $180,000+ in annual fines and stockouts.
What We Built: An AI-native inventory operating system combining high-density real-time data grids with an explainable AI stock rebalancing governor (60s undo window + dynamic safety interlock) and 54px touch-friendly warehouse tablet modes.

3. AI Integration Log (Top 3 Prompts & 1 Failed Prompt Lesson)

Top 3 Time-Saving Prompts (Saved 13+ Hours)
1. Component Variant Matrix Generator:
"Output a full markdown table listing every unique combination of Button [Hierarchy] × [Size] × [State] with exact auto-layout padding, corner radii, and token bindings."
2. DeepSeek Edge Case Generator:
"Brainstorm 10 realistic supply-chain edge cases (API timeout, negative CSV quantities) with plain-English user recovery copy."
3. Microsoft HAX Compliance Audit:
"Audit our AI stock drawer against HAX guidelines G1, G8, G9, G11. Suggest concrete Shape of AI patterns for failures."
The 1 Failed Prompt Lesson (Where AI Failed)
Automated WCAG Color Palette Generation:

We prompted LLMs to generate a high-contrast accessible color palette. The model outputted #64748B on white and claimed it passed WCAG AA. In reality, actual contrast checkers measured 4.4:1 (Fail), and alert text measured 2.1:1 (Fail).

Lesson: Never trust LLMs with math, color luminance, or physics calculations. Mandatory human verification using WebAIM tools is required. We manually hardened tokens to #475569 (7.3:1 AAA Pass).

5. Reflections: Top 3 Learnings & 1 Major Challenge Overcome

Top 3 Learnings
  • Tokens Over Pixels: Semantic variables allowed remediating 12 contrast bugs in 20 minutes across 15+ screens.
  • AI is a Synthesizer, Not an Architect: AI excels at combinatorial matrices, but lacks human restraint and empathy.
  • Enterprise Users Crave Density: E-commerce operators reject spacious whitespace in favor of monospace tabular data rows.
Major Challenge Overcome

Overcoming the AI "Black-Box" Trust Barrier: Operators initially feared auto-rebalance tools would silently deplete stock. We introduced the 3-Tier AI Governor (inline formula transparency, physical slider safety locks when reserve <15%, and a 60s reversible undo toast), increasing SUS from 71.5 to 89.0 (Grade A+).
