2. Six Empirical Observations: Why Hand-Built Layouts Won

1. Spatial Density & Fitts's Law
AI defaulted to consumer e-commerce card grids with 32px whitespace, showing only 3 products. Hand-built high-density 40px table rows display 12+ SKUs simultaneously, slashing scanning time by 65%.
2. Multi-Channel Blindspot
AI modeled inventory as a single static integer. Hand-built architecture segments stock into Shopify, TikTok, Amazon FBA, and Wholesale Reserve—directly solving the core sync crisis.
3. Triage vs. Accounting Models
AI prompted users to classify stock cuts as "Damaged" or "Lost". Hand-built wireframes designed a tactical Source-to-Target reallocation slider with 1-click deficit fill targets.
4. Explainable AI vs. Black-Box
AI provided zero rationale for reorders. Hand-built layout introduced an inline XAI breakdown showing (Velocity × Lead Time) + Safety Buffer = PO, resolving operator distrust.
5. Warehouse Tablet Ergonomics
AI used tiny 28px buttons. Hand-built tablet views enforced 48px+ touch targets and high-contrast status signifiers for operators wearing warehouse work gloves.
6. Error Prevention (Poka-Yoke)
AI assumed 100% happy paths. Hand-built screens included client-side CSV linting, trailing space warnings, and a persistent 60-second floating Undo Banner.

3. Two Documented Peer-Review Iteration Rounds

Iteration Round 1: Wholesale Reserve Depletion Safeguard
Feedback from Alex Vance (Senior Product Designer): "In MOD-02, a user moving the slider to 100% can accidentally drain the wholesale reserve, breaking contract commitments. There is no visual friction."
Layout Evolution: Added a dynamic reserve depletion progress bar (shifting from green to yellow at 50% and red at 80%) plus an explicit 75% Safety Lock Toggle: *"Acknowledge wholesale reserve drop below 150 units"*.
Iteration Round 2: Tablet Status Discriminability & Warehouse Touch Padding
Feedback from Dave K. (Warehouse Systems Logistics Engineer): "Status dots for 'Staged' and 'Pending' look identical on low-cost warehouse tablets. Floor pickers with gloves will miss 32px buttons."
Layout Evolution: Enlarged all tablet action targets to 54px with 16px thumb padding, and replaced circular dots with distinct geometric badges (Hexagon = Synced, Triangle = At-Risk, Square = Blocked).
