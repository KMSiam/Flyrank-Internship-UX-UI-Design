1. The 3-Persona Pack (Grounded Archetypes)

Elena Rostova
The Scrappy Solo Founder · Botanica Glow (29)
"I spent 14 straight hours manually copying order IDs crying over my keyboard. I just need a screen that stops me selling products I can't deliver."
Context & Tech Stack
  • $1.4M ARR DTC Skincare; 8 SKUs.
  • Shopify Plus, Amazon FBA, TikTok Shop, Sheets.
Goals
  • Prevent overselling during viral surges.
  • Cut daily stock audit from 2.5h to 20m.
Frustrations
  • Channel stock sync lags up to 4 hours.
  • Amazon rating penalization from backorders.
Marcus Chen
Warehouse Ops Lead · Apex 3PL (34)
"If the UI doesn't work on a tablet with greasy fingers in a noisy warehouse, it's useless to my team."
Context & Tech Stack
  • Manages 42 pickers across 65,000 sq ft.
  • Zebra Scanners, Rugged iPads, HighJump WMS.
Goals
  • Maintain 99.8% pick accuracy on floor.
  • Zero floor halts from bad client CSV imports.
Frustrations
  • Trailing spaces in SKU codes crashing imports.
  • Tiny unclickable buttons on mobile tablets.
Priya Patel
Senior Demand Planner · Horizon Apparel (31)
"If an AI tool says 'Reorder 500 units', I will not click it without seeing the velocity formula... I revert to Excel."
Context & Tech Stack
  • $8.2M ARR Apparel; 140 SKUs overseas.
  • NetSuite ERP, Looker, Excel PowerQuery.
Goals
  • 98.5% in-stock rate on top 20% core revenue SKUs.
  • Reduce deadstock working capital by 15%.
Frustrations
  • Black-box automated recommendations.
  • Unpredictable shipping delays (6 to 14 weeks).

2. AI First-Draft vs. Human Verification Log

All personas were verified against verbatim transcripts to identify and eliminate synthetic hallucinations.

Area / Archetype DeepSeek AI First-Draft Verified Human Correction Audit Rationale
Elena Tech Stack Stated Elena uses enterprise "SAP ERP and Salesforce Commerce Cloud". Corrected to: Shopify Plus, TikTok Shop Seller Center, Amazon Seller Central, Google Sheets. Real small founders do not use $100k+ enterprise SAP.
Marcus Hardware Suggested Marcus operates from a "MacBook Pro M3 Max at a standing desk". Corrected to: Ruggedized Zebra/Honeywell barcode scanners & cart-mounted iPads. Floor ops require touch-first ergonomics and industrial durability.
Priya Quote Fabricated quote: "I love automated one-click AI ordering because AI knows best." Corrected to: "If an AI says 'Reorder 500 units', I will not click it without seeing the velocity formula." AI hallucinated enthusiasm; raw transcript proved high skepticism.
Journey Curve Plotted a naive upward linear emotional progression from Stage 1 to Stage 6. Corrected to: Volatile curve plunging to -5 (acute panic during oversold viral events). Real fulfillment crises cause severe anxiety prior to resolution.

3. Emotional Journey Map: Multi-Channel Stockout Crisis & Emergency Reroute

Stage Actions Touchpoints User Thoughts Score Pain Points Design Opportunities
1. Alert Trigger Receives urgent mobile push notification on stock deficit. Mobile SMS, iOS Push, Smart Watch. "Is this a glitch or are we actually blowing through stock?" -2 (Anxious) Alerts buried in notification spam. Unified webhook push with high-priority audio chime.
2. Assessment Opens laptop; compares Shopify, TikTok, and Amazon Seller tabs. Chrome browser tabs, Google Sheets. "450 unfulfilled orders on TikTok! Amazon will suspend my account!" -5 (Panic) Mismatched SKU codes across channels; manual calculations. Universal SKU Aliasing Engine with live deficit tally.
3. Triage Uses FlyCommerce OS to model cross-channel virtual inventory rebalance. FlyCommerce OS Modal, XAI Formula Tooltip. "Show me the exact safety buffer math before I click confirm." -1 (Cautious) Fear of black-box automated transfers. Explainable AI (XAI) slider showing stock before/after impact.
4. Warehouse Run Approves 200 unit reallocation; sends pick-wave to Marcus at 3PL. Warehouse Cart Tablet, Zebra Scanner. "Marcus needs clean data before 5 PM cut-off. No errors." +3 (Focused) Malformed CSV uploads causing floor work stoppages. Pre-flight CSV data linter with 48px touch targets.
5. Customer Care System triggers automated transactional emails with 10% coupon. Klaviyo Email API, TikTok Messaging. "Keep buyers informed before they leave negative reviews." +4 (Relieved) Generic cancellation messages sparking chargebacks. Dynamic transactional delay notification templates.
6. Prevention Priya configures automated dynamic safety buffers for TikTok surges. FlyCommerce Rule Engine, Analytics. "We are never losing an entire weekend to inventory triage again." +5 (Resilient) Manual rule management forgotten after crisis ends. "One-Click Preventative Rule" suggested post-incident.

4. "How to Use This" Guide for Product Managers

Operational Implementation Framework for PMs & Engineering Sprints
1. The Elena Test (Speed)
Any core operational triage task must be executable in under 3 clicks / 90 seconds. Eliminate deep configuration submenus.
2. The Marcus Test (Ergonomics)
All warehouse UI components must have minimum 48px touch targets and pre-validated input sanitization to prevent floor freezes.
3. The Priya Test (Transparency)
No AI recommendation is displayed without an Explainable AI breakdown (Velocity + Lead Time + Buffer) and 1-click Excel export.
