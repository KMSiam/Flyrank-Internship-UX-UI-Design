2. Defense & Changelog of Eliminated UI Elements (-44% Cut)

Eliminated UI Element Action Why It Was Safe to Remove (Written Defense) Law of UX Cited
Conversational AI Chatbox CUT #1 Typing open-ended text during a panic introduces high cognitive latency and non-deterministic LLM response delays. Tactile sliders with quick-snaps are 5x faster. Hick's Law & Doherty Threshold
30-Day Velocity Scrubber Chart CUT #2 Emergency stock reallocation requires only a 48-hour stopgap. Historical multi-week trends do not alter immediate warehouse deficits; saved 25h of charting dev. Miller's Law (Chunking)
Live TikTok Views & Sentiment CUT #3 Vanity metrics that create emotional dread without offering logistics utility. Physical shelf stock is fixed regardless of live stream viewer counts. Visual Noise Reduction
Competitor Price Tracker CUT #4 Dynamic repricing belongs in offline merchandising, not high-stakes out-of-stock crisis triage. Mental Model Separation
6-Channel International Matrix CUT #5 92% of sales and 100% of viral events occur in the US domestic market. International sub-allocations can be configured post-crisis. Pareto Principle (80/20)
Carrier Shipping Rate Calculator CUT #6 Freight rates are fixed under annual 3PL contracts. Calculating shipping postage inside an inventory buffer reservation modal conflates fulfillment with triage. Separation of Concerns
Monte Carlo Simulation Curve CUT #7 Academic bloat. Operators need an actionable buffer number (14d + 4d safety), not stochastic probability density curves. Occam's Razor
In-Modal Email & SMS Composer CUT #8 Customer delay notices are automated via background Klaviyo webhooks; composing messages inside an emergency modal induces operational delay. Automation of Routine Tasks
Triplicate Export Formats (XLSX/PDF) CUT #9 Consolidating down to a single "Export Audit CSV" satisfies all data verification requirements while cleaning the button bar. Hick's Law
