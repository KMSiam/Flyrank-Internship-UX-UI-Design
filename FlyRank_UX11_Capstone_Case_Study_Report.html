1. The Problem & User Discovery

Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) brands scaling from $2M to $20M GMV across Shopify, TikTok Shop, and Amazon FBA suffer from a catastrophic 42-minute batch inventory sync lag. When viral spikes occur on TikTok Shop, inventory runs dry; yet Shopify and Amazon continue accepting orders for phantom stock. This triggers stockouts, customer churn, seller rating bans, and over $180,000 in annual emergency freight penalties.

"I don't need a black-box AI that silently re-routes $50,000 of stock behind my back. I need transparent velocity metrics, 60-second reversible safeguards, and touch-friendly actions I can execute on an iPad cart in a loud warehouse."
— Maya Lin, Head of E-Commerce Supply Chain Operations (Primary Persona)

2. The Strategic Pivot & IA Flow Architecture

Strategic Pivot ("Kill Your Darlings")

In early wireframes (UX-04A), we designed a complex 3D interactive warehouse bin visualizer. User testing proved operators found it slow, distracting, and unviable under poor warehouse Wi-Fi.

Pivot: Cut the 3D visualizer in favor of a high-density, sub-second data table paired with a 1-click predictive rebalance drawer.
End-to-End User Flow Arc

Trigger: Automated velocity detector flags TikTok spike (6.2h runway remaining).
Inspect: Operator opens AI governor drawer with live formula transparency.
Act: 1-click approval creates warehouse transfer slips with a 60-second undo countdown.

4. Designing the AI Surface: Trust & Human Governance

Shape of AI Pattern Implementation in FlyCommerce OS User Trust & Safety Mechanism
Reversible Action Plan (Governor) Stock transfer drawer displays before/after balances across all channels prior to execution. 60-second visual countdown with instant 1-click cancellation to prevent remorse.
Formula Disclosure (Trust Builder) Inline card explicating calculation: (TikTok Velocity × 1.4) - Transit Lag. No black-box opacity; operators understand exactly which historical sales window was parsed.
Graceful Degradation (Failure Mode) Ambiguity banner when marketplace APIs lag or experience rate limits. System expands safety stock buffers by +15% automatically and flags reduced confidence.

5. Usability Testing & Top 3 Iterations (UX-09)

Moderated think-aloud sessions with 5 enterprise operations leads surfaced 8 defects; top 3 were remediated immediately:

Observed Friction Severity Prototype Iteration Shipped Post-Iteration Outcome
Excess Reserve Rebalance P0 Critical Added dynamic depletion gauge; turns amber <15% and locks transfer if buffer hits 0. 0 over-depletions across re-tests.
Hidden Formula Logic P1 Major Elevated formula from hover tooltip into a persistent inline card with confidence score. Hesitation dropped from 48s to 6s.
Warehouse Tablet Mis-Taps P1 Major Enlarged action targets to 54px full-width touch areas on warehouse tablet view. 100% first-tap accuracy achieved.

6. AI Workflow Reflection: What Worked, What Failed & What Stayed Human

Where AI Failed (Required Human Fixes)
  • Contrast Hallucinations: Claude suggested color tokens that failed WCAG AA (4.4:1). Human remediation was required to achieve 7.3:1 AAA.
  • Dashboard Bloat: AI wireframes defaulted to 14 useless visual charts, missing the core triage workflow.
What Stayed 100% Human
  • Empathy with Operator Panic: Understanding the terror of Amazon seller rating penalties cannot be simulated.
  • Moderating Usability Nuance: Detecting subtle sighs, hesitation pauses, and unspoken user uncertainty.

7. Critical Reflections

One Decision I Would Defend
High-Density Data Grid over "Spacious Card Tiles": While modern Dribbble shots favor cards with massive padding, operators triaging 4,000 SKUs need immediate vertical scanability and tabular numbers. This choice cut task time by 72%.
One Thing I Would Do Differently
Simulate Warehouse Environmental Friction Earlier: Testing on quiet desktops masked touch-target and glare issues until Week 6. In the future, I will simulate noisy warehouse conditions and tablet grips during initial low-fi wireframing.
