1. Interaction Model Justification (Agent vs. Chatbot vs. Autopilot)

Selected Model: Proactive Surveillance Agent with Staged Human Review.
• Why Chatbots Were Rejected: Natural language typing during a high-stakes stockout crisis adds cognitive latency (>2,000ms), ambiguity in SKU syntax, and buries multi-channel numbers in wordy chat bubbles.
• Why Full Autopilot Was Rejected: 68% of operators abandon automated tools that reallocate stock or issue purchase orders without explicit consent (UX-02 Finding 2).

2. Four Named Patterns from "The Shape of AI" Library

Governor Pattern
1. Action Plan (Staged Execution)
Decomposes AI proposals into an explicit 3-step execution plan (Step 1: Reserve 150u from Wholesale; Step 2: Push TikTok lock; Step 3: Transmit 3PL pick manifest). Operators can toggle individual steps before execution.
Governor Pattern
2. Interactive Verification Slider
Tactile reallocation slider with hard safety locks. If the user drags past 75% of total wholesale reserve, the system stops and requires an explicit checkbox confirmation to prevent accidental stock depletion.
Trust Builder
3. Explainable Formula Disclosure
Replaces black-box "Smart AI" badges with an inspectable math formula: (Velocity [14.2u/h] × 48h Buffer) + Lead Time = 136 Units, directly addressing operator skepticism.
Trust Builder
4. Telemetry Citations & Caveats
Cites data recency for every number (Shopify sync 42s ago, TikTok 7d velocity) alongside active supplier caveats: "⚠️ Factory Blackout in 18 days for Lunar New Year (14d buffer appended)".

3. AI Failure & Uncertainty Mode: Split-Scenario Branching

⚠️ Anomaly Detected: 16x Viral Sales Surge (Confidence: 48% — Below Automated Action Threshold)
The AI cannot determine if this surge is transient or permanent. Rather than failing silently or throwing a red error toast, it surfaces two divergent scenario models:
Scenario A: Transient Flash Spike
• Rebalance 150 buffer units from wholesale pool.
• Do NOT issue international supplier PO.
Capital at Risk: $0 (Internal Buffer Shift)
Scenario B: Sustained Demand Shift
• Rebalance 300 units from FBA hub.
• Issue emergency Air-Freight Supplier PO.
Capital at Risk: $24,500 (Production Commit)

4. Microsoft HAX Guidelines Audit (Human-AI Interaction)

HAX Guideline Status Implementation Evidence in FlyCommerce OS Audit Finding & Planned Fix
G1: Make clear what system can do PASS Onboarding modal scopes jurisdiction strictly to inventory monitoring and buffer planning. Clear system mental model.
G2: Make clear how well it can do it PASS Every recommendation explicitly displays its confidence score (e.g. "94% Confidence"). Prevents over-reliance on AI.
G4: Show contextually relevant info PASS Telemetry (velocity, lead times, factory blackouts) shown directly adjacent to interaction. Eliminates menu navigation.
G8: Support efficient dismissal PASS Proactive warning toasts have an instant single-click 4-hour snooze button. Preserves user attention.
G9: Support efficient correction PASS Tactile allocation slider enables instant override of AI-suggested units in <2s. Zero friction correction.
G11: Make clear why system acted PASS Pattern 3 (Explainable Formula Drawer) exposes exact arithmetic formula. Full algorithmic transparency.
G10: Scope services when in doubt HONEST FAIL Early prototypes computed exaggerated reorders during viral spikes, confusing users. Planned Fix Implemented: Split-Scenario Uncertainty Modal triggered when confidence <70%.
G13: Learn from user behavior HONEST FAIL If Elena repeatedly overrides the 15% buffer to 25%, the system forgot her preference. Planned Fix Implemented: Adaptive Memory Token prompts default update after 3 overrides.
