2. NOTES / CONTEXT FOR REVIEWERS:
Assignment UX-04 (Wireframes: AI Draft vs Hand-Built) Summary:

1. 12-SCREEN LOW-FIDELITY WIREFRAME SUITE (Traced to UX-03 IA):
   - Primary Task Flow (Emergency Triage):
     * SCR-01: Unified Multi-Channel Stock Grid (High-density 1440px desktop grid)
     * SCR-02: SKU Detail & Channel Aliasing Drawer
     * SCR-04: Emergency Stockout Triage Center
     * MOD-02: Cross-Channel Virtual Rebalancing Modal (Tactile slider + quick-snaps)
     * MOD-01: Explainable AI (XAI) Formula Inspector Modal
   - Onboarding & First-Run Flow:
     * WIZ-01: Multi-Store OAuth Connection Stepper (Shopify, TikTok Shop, Amazon FBA)
     * WIZ-02: Catalog Ingestion Progress Stepper
     * WIZ-03: Automated SKU Auto-Match & Conflict Resolver
     * WIZ-04: Baseline Buffer Strategy Calibration (15% dynamic buffer activation)
   - Recovery & Warehouse Execution Flow:
     * MOD-03: Pre-Flight CSV Upload & In-Browser Linting Modal
     * MOD-04: CSV Trailing Space & Syntax Warning Drawer
     * BAN-01: Persistent 60-Second Floating Undo Banner & Rollback

2. AI DRAFT VS. HAND-BUILT EXPERIMENT:
   Tested Figma AI / First Draft against hand-crafted designs on two key screens:
   - Screen 1 (SCR-01 Stock Grid):
     * AI Output: Consumer e-commerce card layout, 32px whitespace, retail price tags, destructive delete buttons. Only 3 items visible per viewport.
     * Hand-Built: High-density 8pt data table showing 12+ SKUs per viewport, multi-channel split (Shopify/TikTok/FBA/Reserve), and persistent sync health header.
   - Screen 2 (MOD-02 Rebalance Modal):
     * AI Output: Generic accounting adjustments ("Damaged Stock") treating inventory as a single static number.
     * Hand-Built: Dynamic Source-to-Target routing, tactile slider with quick-snaps, Explainable AI math formula, and factory blackout calendar warnings.

3. 6 IN-DEPTH OBSERVATIONS:
   - Obs 1 (Spatial Density / Fitts's Law): AI's low-density consumer cards increase triage time by 300%; hand-built 40px table rows maximize scanning velocity.
   - Obs 2 (Multi-Channel Blindspot): AI modeled inventory as a single static number; hand-built architecture captures multi-channel segmentation.
   - Obs 3 (Triage vs Accounting): AI defaulted to damage write-offs; hand-built focused on virtual buffer routing.
   - Obs 4 (Explainable AI): AI gave black-box outputs; hand-built exposed the underlying sales velocity and lead-time formula.
   - Obs 5 (Warehouse Tablet Ergonomics): AI used microscopic 28px buttons; hand-built applied 48px+ touch targets for gloved warehouse operators.
   - Obs 6 (Unhappy Paths): AI assumed 100% happy paths; hand-built introduced client-side CSV linting and 60-second undo rollbacks.

4. TWO DOCUMENTED PEER-REVIEW ITERATIONS:
   - Round 1 (Senior Designer Alex): Added dynamic reserve depletion health bar and 75% safety lock toggle to prevent accidental complete reserve drain.
   - Round 2 (Logistics Engineer Dave): Enlarged tablet touch targets to 54px and converted status tags to distinct geometric shapes (Hexagon=Synced, Triangle=At-Risk, Square=Blocked) for warehouse legibility.
