Hi Team,

Here is my submission for the Capstone Impact Project (Code: UX · 24h Workload) summarizing the entire 8-week product design journey for FlyCommerce OS.

Key Sections in the Impact Summary:
1. Project Snapshot:
   - Problem: 42-minute batch inventory sync lag between Shopify, TikTok Shop, and Amazon FBA costs merchants $180k/year in stockout and overselling penalties.
   - Solution: Predictive inventory operating system with real-time velocity balancing and an explainable AI governor drawer.
   - 3 Key Outcomes:
     • System Usability Scale (SUS) score jumped from 71.5 to 89.0 (Grade A+ Excellent / Top 5% percentile).
     • Time-to-Execute Stock Balancing dropped by 72% (from 3m 12s to 54s).
     • 100% WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance across all screens with zero detached tokens.

2. Week-by-Week Journey Map (W1 to W8):
   - Mapped all 12 key deliverables and specific AI tooling (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, DeepSeek-R1, Recraft, Relume, Playwright).

3. AI Integration Log (Top 3 Prompts & 1 Failed Prompt):
   - Prompt 1: Combinatorial Component Variant Matrix Generator (Saved ~6 hours).
   - Prompt 2: DeepSeek-R1 Supply Chain Edge Case Stress-Tester (Saved ~4 hours).
   - Prompt 3: Microsoft HAX Compliance Auditor (Saved ~3 hours).
   - Failed Prompt: Automated WCAG color generation hallucinated that #64748B passed AA; real contrast checkers measured 4.4:1 (Fail). Lesson learned: never trust LLMs for math/luminance; enforced mandatory WebAIM human audits.

4. Key Shipped Assets Catalog:
   - Master Figma File: 6 organized pages (Tokens, Variables, Components, 15+ Hi-Fi Screens, Redlines).
   - Interactive Prototype: 3 named user journeys with Smart Animate and dynamic safety interlocks.
   - Developer Hand-Off Redlines: Detailed spacing, box model, focus rings, and production SVGs.

5. Reflections:
   - Top 3 Learnings: Tokens over pixels; AI is a synthesizer not an architect; enterprise users crave high density over whitespace.
   - Major Challenge Overcome: Overcoming the "black-box AI trust barrier" by engineering the 3-Tier AI Governor (formula disclosure, 15% safety stock lock, and 60s reversible undo toast).
