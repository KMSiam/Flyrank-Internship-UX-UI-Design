2. CAPSTONE PROJECT PITCH (FlyCommerce OS - DTC Inventory & Fulfillment Hub):
   - User: Solo direct-to-consumer (DTC) e-commerce founders and operations managers running multi-channel stores.
   - Problem: Costly stockouts and manual order reconciliation across Shopify, Amazon, and TikTok Shop causing an estimated 18% revenue loss.
   - Platform: Responsive web application and companion tablet-optimized fulfillment dashboard.
   - Measurable Success Criterion: 45% reduction in daily inventory reconciliation time and a 95% first-time completion rate on order rerouting workflows during usability testing.
   - Why You: A systems-first design methodology combining high-density data clarity, scalable atomic design systems, and rapid AI-assisted iteration.
