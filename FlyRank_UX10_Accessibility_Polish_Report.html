1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Accessibility failures are the primary cause for enterprise SaaS procurement blocks and engineering sprint retro-fits. Correcting contrast ratios or keyboard traps directly in Figma design system variables costs minutes; remediating hard-coded CSS post-release costs entire engineering quarters. This pass conducts a rigorous 12-point WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA audit across all high-fidelity screens of FlyCommerce OS, resolves every failure at the root variable level, and executes a full design system polish sweep.

4. Full Consistency Sweep: Spacing, Typography & Radii

1. 8pt Spacing Grid
100% of auto-layout frames bound to 4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px. Eliminated all odd-number margins.
2. Modular Typography
Major Third 1.250 modular scale enforced across 6 styles. Tabular numerals (tnum) enabled for numerical stock counters.
3. Geometric Corner Radii
Enforced 5 semantic tokens: 4px (Badges), 6px (Form Inputs), 8px (Buttons), 12px (Cards), 16px (Modals & Drawers).
4. Icon Bounding Boxes
Standardized 3 bounding tiers: 16px (Inline/Badges), 20px (Action Fields), 24px (Navigation Bar). Optical center aligned.
